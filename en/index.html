About

I am a professor at Universidade do Vale do Rio dos Sinos (Unisinos), in São Leopoldo, Brazil, where I have taught since 2013. I work in undergraduate programs, specialization courses and the Applied Computing Graduate Program (PPGCA), and I am a member of the Graduate Studies, Research and Extension Chamber of the University Council.

I have more than 25 years of professional experience in information technology, as a systems analyst, technical leader, software development manager and project manager, at organizations such as ASAV, Processor, HCPA (Hospital de Clínicas de Porto Alegre, through CTIS), Dell (through the Eldorado Research Institute), Advanced IT, Qualità Informática and Pioneer Hi-Bred International, as well as the systems area of Unisinos itself. This experience grounds my approach to teaching and research, oriented toward real problems and applicable solutions.

My research focuses on software engineering and applied artificial intelligence, with emphasis on digital health, and on the training of professionals and researchers in the field.

Research

The research applies software engineering and artificial intelligence methods to problems in healthcare, business and industry, evaluating performance, cost, scalability and quality, in projects with partners and real data.

Supervision topics

  • Artificial intelligence applied to complex systems

    Machine learning and deep learning, language models and agentic artificial intelligence.

  • Software engineering and intelligent environments

    Quality, reliability, scalable architectures, modern engineering practices and intelligent environments.

  • Distributed systems and interoperability in digital health

    Blockchain, Internet of Things, fog computing and open standards such as HL7 FHIR and openEHR.

  • Application domains

    Digital health, business, industry and software systems aimed at social impact.

Supervision

Supervision of master's students and co-supervision of doctoral students, as well as undergraduate research and final projects.

Projects

Ongoing research and development projects, conducted in partnership with laboratories, companies and funding agencies.

  • Coordination

    OmniSER

    Personal Social-Engagement Record. Integrated computing platform to support social action, aimed at people in vulnerable situations, combining artificial intelligence, georeferenced data, blockchain and interoperability to standardize information from multiple sources and support coordinated actions.

  • SoftwareLab

    Projects with Dell

    Applied research on agentic artificial intelligence, small language models and software engineering for integrated digital experiences.

  • SoftwareLab

    INTEROPCHAIN-Saúde.BR

    Interoperability in consent management and in the intelligent analysis of health data on blockchain, integrating the HL7 FHIR standard with the Hyperledger Fabric network. An initiative of the Brazilian National Research and Education Network (RNP), through its Digital Health Collaboration Networks Program, with the participation of IFSul.

  • VizLab

    IG2

    Integration of geological and geophysical data in a virtual reality environment. A VizLab project funded by Petrobras.

  • VizLab

    VizLab Academy

    Coordination of the VizLab training program, linked to the IG2 project, with internal courses on software development, graphics engines and extended reality.

Completed projects: MinhaSaúdeDigital (2020 to 2025), MinhaHistóriaDigital (2021 to 2023), interoperable blockchain-based model for electronic health records within uHospital (2018 to 2021), Hospital Rooms of the Future (2017 to 2020) and uHospital (2015 to 2018).
