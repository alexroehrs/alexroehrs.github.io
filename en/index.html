About

I am a professor at Universidade do Vale do Rio dos Sinos (Unisinos), in São Leopoldo, Brazil, where I have taught since 2013. Today I work in undergraduate programs, specializations and the Applied Computing Graduate Program (PPGCA).

Before and alongside my academic career, I built more than 25 years of professional experience in software engineering and technology management. That background shapes how I teach and do research: focused on real problems and on solutions that can be implemented.

My current research centers on applied artificial intelligence, with emphasis on digital health, and on preparing people to work with AI in industry and academia.

Research

I work on topics that bring artificial intelligence and software engineering closer to applications with direct impact on people and organizations.

AI applied to healthcare

Machine learning models for diagnostic support, medical image analysis and digital health.

Language models and agentic AI

Efficient adaptation of language models and AI agent architectures for real tasks.

Health data interoperability

Personal health records, interoperability standards and blockchain for distributed data.

Software engineering and distributed systems

Cloud, fog and edge architectures, serverless computing and intelligent environments.

Visualization and virtual reality

Integration and visualization of scientific data in immersive environments.

AI in business and industry

Applying AI to processes, products and decisions in organizations.
