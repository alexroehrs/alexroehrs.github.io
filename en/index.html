About

I am a professor at Universidade do Vale do Rio dos Sinos (Unisinos), in São Leopoldo, Brazil, where I have taught since 2013. I work in undergraduate programs, specialization courses and the Applied Computing Graduate Program (PPGCA).

I have more than 25 years of professional experience in software engineering and technology management, which grounds my approach to teaching and research, oriented toward real problems and applicable solutions.

My research focuses on applied artificial intelligence, with emphasis on digital health, and on the training of professionals and researchers in the field.

Research

The research lines bring together artificial intelligence and software engineering in applications with impact on people and organizations.

  • Artificial intelligence applied to healthcare

    Machine learning models for diagnostic support, medical image analysis and digital health.

  • Language models and agentic artificial intelligence

    Efficient adaptation of language models and architectures of artificial intelligence agents.

  • Health data interoperability

    Personal health records, interoperability standards and blockchain for distributed data.

  • Software engineering and distributed systems

    Cloud, fog and edge architectures, serverless computing and intelligent environments.

  • Visualization and virtual reality

    Integration and visualization of scientific data in immersive environments.

  • Artificial intelligence in business and industry

    Application of artificial intelligence to organizational processes, products and decisions.
