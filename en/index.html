Research

The research applies software engineering and artificial intelligence methods to problems in healthcare, business and industry, evaluating performance, cost, scalability and quality, in projects with partners and real data.

Supervision topics

  • Distributed systems and interoperability in digital health

    Blockchain, Internet of Things, fog computing and open standards such as HL7 FHIR and openEHR.

  • Software engineering

    Quality, reliability, scalable architectures and modern engineering practices.

  • Artificial intelligence applied to complex systems

    Machine learning and deep learning, language models, agentic artificial intelligence and intelligent environments.

  • Application domains

    Digital health, business, industry and software systems aimed at social impact.

Research lines at PPGCA: Distributed Applications and Communication Infrastructure; Software Engineering and Intelligent Environments; Artificial Intelligence Applied to Healthcare, Business and Industry.

Supervision

Supervision of master's students and co-supervision of doctoral students, as well as undergraduate research and final projects.

About

I am a professor at Universidade do Vale do Rio dos Sinos (Unisinos), in São Leopoldo, Brazil, where I have taught since 2013. I work in undergraduate programs, specialization courses and the Applied Computing Graduate Program (PPGCA), and I am a member of the Graduate Studies, Research and Extension Chamber of the University Council. At the undergraduate level, I have supervised 39 final projects.

I have more than 25 years of professional experience in information technology, as a systems analyst, technical leader, software development manager and project manager, at organizations such as ASAV, Processor, HCPA (Hospital de Clínicas de Porto Alegre, through CTIS), Dell (through the Eldorado Research Institute), Advanced IT, Qualità Informática and Pioneer Hi-Bred International, as well as the systems area of Unisinos itself. This experience grounds my approach to teaching and research, oriented toward real problems and applicable solutions.

My research focuses on applied artificial intelligence, with emphasis on digital health, and on the training of professionals and researchers in the field.
